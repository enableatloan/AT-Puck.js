Using Puck.js as Assistive Technology

The Puck.js is an open-source Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) smart button that can be programmed to perform a wide range of functions. Its flexibility makes it a valuable tool for creating customised assistive technology solutions.

Getting Started

Setting up the Puck.js is straightforward. Using the online Espruino IDE, you can wirelessly upload JavaScript code directly to the device and configure it to perform specific actions.

The Puck.js is particularly useful for simplifying complex keyboard shortcuts that may be difficult or impossible for some users to access using a standard keyboard.

The Puck.js can also be programmed to send multi-key combinations such as Ctrl + Alt + Delete. To do this:

Connect the Puck.js to the online Espruino IDE.
Upload the required script.
Disconnect the device from the IDE.
Pair the Puck.js directly with your computer via Bluetooth.

Once paired, a single press of the Puck.js can trigger the entire key sequence automatically.

Advantages
Low-power wireless operation.
Compact and highly portable.
Fully programmable and customizable.
Battery life can last up to a year under typical use.
Can emulate keyboard shortcuts and other Bluetooth Human Interface Device (HID) functions.
Limitations
The integrated switch can be difficult to activate reliably.
Users may need to press a specific area of the top surface to achieve consistent operation.
Some individuals may require an external switch solution for easier access.
Verdict

The Puck.js has significant potential as a versatile assistive technology tool. Its programmability, wireless connectivity, and Bluetooth HID capabilities make it suitable for a wide range of accessibility applications, from computer access to environmental control and communication support.
